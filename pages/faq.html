How much are my assessments to the condominium association for my unit type and when are they due?
Assessments range from $516.08 to $1719.03 per month and are based on square footage. Refer to _ to determine your unit type and corresponding square footage. Payments are due on the 000th day of each month.
Do I have to be a member in any other association? If so, what is the name of the association and what are my voting rights in this association? How much are my assessments?
No. You do not need to be a member of any other association to be a member of the Waterside at Coquina Key South condominium association.
Am I required to pay rent or land-use fees for recreational or other commonly used facilities? If so, how much am I obligated to pay annually?
No. Your unit is part of the Waterside at Coquina Key South condominium association, and you are not required to pay rent or land-use fees for recreational or other commonly used facilities.
Is the condominium association or other mandatory membership association involved in any court cases in which it may face liability in excess of $100,000? If so, identify each such case.
No. The Waterside at Coquina Key South condominium association is not involved in any court cases in which it may face liability in excess of $100,000.
